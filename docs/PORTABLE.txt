CORTA 1.0.0 — Windows x64

Extract the entire folder, then open Corta.exe.
Keep the executable together with the other files.

Open or drop an audio file. Drag the purple handles. Play. Save.
Choose the original filename in the save dialog to replace it.
The editing timeline continues to refer to the originally loaded audio.

ES / EN changes the language and remembers your choice.
Ctrl+O opens audio. Space plays/pauses. Escape stops.

Edit textos.json beside the executable to customize wording, then restart.
Translations live under locales.es and locales.en.

Extraé toda la carpeta y abrí Corta.exe.
Abrí o arrastrá un audio. Marcá el recorte. Reproducí. Guardá.
Para reemplazar el original, elegí ese mismo archivo al guardar.
El switch ES / EN cambia y recuerda el idioma.
